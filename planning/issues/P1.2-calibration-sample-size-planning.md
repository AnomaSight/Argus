## Summary
Functions that say how many normal images a target FPR needs, and the lowest target a given sample supports.

## Why this matters
Stops anyone from claiming a 1% FPR guarantee from 150 images. Used in configs, the protocol doc and the write-up.

## What to do
1. `min_normals(alpha, delta) = ceil(log δ / log(1 − α))`.
2. `min_alpha(m, delta) = 1 − δ^(1/m)`.
3. `planning_table(alphas, m, delta)` → the Table 6 layout (rank, achieved confidence, minimum m, supported?).
