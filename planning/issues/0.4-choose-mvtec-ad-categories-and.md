## Summary
Decide which MVTec AD categories to use and how to divide each one's good images into support, calibration and evaluation-normal sets.

## Why this matters
MVTec's test set has only about 40 good images per category, too few to measure FPR precisely. This decision fixes the statistical power of the whole MVTec half of the project.

## What to do
1. Download MVTec AD (or read its documentation) and record exact counts per category: train good, test good, test defects.
2. Pick categories: hazelnut (many training images) plus at least one where shrinking memory hurt in the ASO paper (screw is the clearest case: -0.7 AUROC). Add more only if compute allows.
3. Per category, split the training good images into `support` / `calib` / `eval_normal`. Starting point for hazelnut (~391 train): ~200 / 100 / rest. Evaluation normals = held-out train goods + all test goods.
4. Check the calibration size against targets: 100 supports 10% and 5%; 2% needs at least 149.
5. ISP-AD: copy Deng's design — per modality (LSM-2, LSM-1, ASM) 300 support, 150 calib, 200 eval normal, plus all real defects.
6. Seeds: 3 seeds for both datasets (reuse Deng's 20260801–20260803 for readability).
7. Write `configs/datasets/mvtec_ad.yaml` and `configs/datasets/isp_ad.yaml` with these numbers.
