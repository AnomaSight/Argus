## Summary
Run Deng's exact 100%-memory PatchCore setup on ISP-AD and check the numbers match his paper.

## Why this matters
Reproducing the published result first shows the ISP-AD pipeline is right before any new memory settings are added.

## What to do
1. Config: WRN-50 layers 2+3, `deng` preset (256×256, no crop), m100, image score = max k = 1 distance (no reweighting).
2. 3 modalities (LSM-2, LSM-1, ASM) × 3 seeds, split sizes 300 / 150 / 200.
3. Calibrate at α = 10%, δ = 5% (rank 142) with `report.py`; compute AUROC, FPR, point-defect recall.

- ISP-AD m100 score files
- Reproduction table vs Deng Table 2 in `docs/results.md`
