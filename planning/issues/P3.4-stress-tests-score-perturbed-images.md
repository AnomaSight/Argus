Produces the data for the stress-test study. Uses finished banks only; no model building.

2. Score with `load_detector(...).score_batch()`; write C2 folders under `artifacts/scores_stress/` (same schema).
3. Keep the original (clean) tau; never recalibrate on perturbed images.
